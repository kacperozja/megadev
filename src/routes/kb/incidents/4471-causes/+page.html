Ticket #4471 was merged as one issue: "dates parse mm/dd in eu locales." It is three.

The three causes

parser (374 reports, 11 tenants): The shared date parser reads every date month-first (MM/DD). Tenants whose locale writes the day first (DD/MM) get day and month swapped in invites and reminders. Affects app and email channels.

hint (33 reports, 3 tenants): Booking form v3 shows MM/DD/YYYY as its date hint. Users in day-first locales follow the hint and type month-first, but the form reads the input in the tenant's locale (day-first). Day and month swap again — a different bug, only on the form channel, only for tenants on booking-v3: alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour.

off-by-one (5 reports, 4 tenants): Bookings land on the adjacent day — one day off, not a month/day swap. The desk read these as a one-day move (desk-notes oneDayMove). Likely a timezone or date-boundary bug: R-41344 shows Lisbon and Toronto getting different days for the same meeting.

These need separate fixes. The parser fix does not help form users who followed the hint, and neither fix addresses the off-by-one timezone issue.

Why the desk missed it

The dedupe rule (decision 0001) merges on subject alone. Reports about both bugs carry subjects like "Date is wrong" and "Month and day swapped?" — the same words, different causes. The channel and the tenant's booking form decide the cause, not the subject.

What to do

File each cause as its own ticket. The parser bug is the larger one; the hint bug needs a form-side fix (either change the hint to match the locale, or change the form to read what the hint says). The off-by-one reports need a timezone investigation.
